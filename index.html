Research

LLM agents on the multi-mode RCPSP

In the MRCPSP every activity can run in one of several modes, each trading duration against renewable and non-renewable resources, under precedence constraints, and the goal is the shortest makespan. I study where agents succeed and fail when they have to build a full solver for it: picking modes, keeping resource usage feasible, and running an anytime search that keeps improving until the time limit.

An executable benchmark task on integrated healthcare timetabling

A task built on the problem of the Integrated Healthcare Timetabling Competition 2024 (IHTC-2024), in preparation for the RSI-Exam benchmark. An agent gets one hour to write a solver that decides patient admissions, operating theaters and nurse assignments together. It is graded on freshly generated hidden instances, against a competition finalist's solver as the reference and a proven lower bound as the ceiling, with a pass threshold set from the reference's run-to-run noise.
